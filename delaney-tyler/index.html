For Delaney Heffner and Tyler Keys, the countdown to midnight on New Year's Eve 2025 came with an unexpected twist: a proposal that had been six years in the making.

Tyler, who has been dating Delaney since 2019, orchestrated an elaborate surprise that went far beyond getting down on one knee. Working in secret with both families, he arranged for relatives to drive into Chicago for what Delaney believed was simply a New Year's Eve celebration.

"Omg thanks friends I am shook," an elated Delaney shared exclusively with PEEPLE after the proposal.

"What am I, a child bride? Not anymore!"

The couple first met while working at the same law firm, where they quickly discovered a connection that would last well beyond their 9-to-5. Six years and one Chicago apartment later, Tyler decided it was finally time to make things official.

The proposal caps off what has been a whirlwind romance that took the couple from coworkers to partners to now, officially, engaged. Friends say the two have been inseparable since they started dating, building a life together in Chicago's trendy Logan Square neighborhood.

And while six years might seem like a long time to wait, Delaney's reaction made clear it was worth every moment. The newly-engaged bride-to-be spent the rest of the evening celebrating with the family members who had secretly gathered to celebrate the magical moment.
